The same 8 encounter events from Part A are shown below for reference. Now put them in the correct chronological order by answering the sequencing questions.

The 8 encounter events — reference list
AThe patient's health plan sends the provider an explanation of payment, showing the allowed amount, adjustments, and patient responsibility.
BThe practice management system queries the health plan to confirm the patient's coverage, deductible status, and copay.
CThe patient's physician determines an MRI is needed and submits clinical documentation to the health plan for approval before scheduling.
DThe provider submits a detailed claim to the health plan listing the diagnoses, procedures performed, and charges.
EThe health plan sends the provider a payment via electronic funds transfer.
FThe health plan approves the MRI request and returns an authorization number.
GThe provider checks the status of a claim submitted three weeks ago that hasn't been paid or denied yet.
HThe patient completes pre-registration online, entering insurance card information.
