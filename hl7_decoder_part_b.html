You decoded an HL7 message
The pipe-delimited format looks intimidating at first, but every segment has a consistent structure — once you know what MSH, PID, PV1, ORC, and OBR mean, you can extract the clinical story from any HL7 v2 order message. In a real integration troubleshooting scenario, this skill — being able to read a raw message and identify what's missing or wrong — is exactly what an integration analyst needs when a lab order fails to appear on the lab's worklist.
